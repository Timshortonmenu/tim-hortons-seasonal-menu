Tim Hortons Seasonal Menu Tracker

Tim Hortons has built a loyal following over the years, partly because of its steady menu staples, but also because of how often it introduces something new. Seasonal and limited-time items tend to generate the most conversation, since they only stick around for a short window before disappearing again. This page keeps a simple record of what's been showing up lately and what's worth paying attention to.

Recent Seasonal Additions

Some of the more talked-about recent releases include the Chocolate Pistachio lineup and TimsBoost energy drinks, both of which brought a noticeably different flavor profile compared to the regular, year-round menu. Since items like these don't tend to last long once they're introduced, checking the latest seasonal lineup every couple of weeks is usually enough to avoid missing out entirely.

How Often Does the Menu Change

Seasonal drops typically happen a handful of times a year, often lining up with a particular season or a promotional push. Once an item is pulled from the menu, there's no real guarantee it'll come back, which is part of why some flavors end up becoming talking points long after they're gone.

Tips for Not Missing Out

A quick check-in every few weeks is generally enough to stay on top of new releases before they're pulled. For anyone planning a visit that isn't just about the seasonal drinks, there's also a quick breakdown of breakfast and bakery picks that tends to pair well with whatever's new at the time.
