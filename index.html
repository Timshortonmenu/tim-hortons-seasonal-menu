Tim Hortons Seasonal Menu Tracker

Tim Hortons regularly rotates its menu with seasonal and limited-time items, making it easy to miss out if you're not paying attention. This page tracks recent additions and what to expect.

Recent Seasonal Additions

Some of the most talked-about recent items include the Chocolate Pistachio lineup and TimsBoost energy drinks — both introduced as limited-time offerings that brought a different flavor experience compared to the regular menu.

How Often Does the Menu Change

Seasonal items typically show up a few times a year, often tied to specific seasons or promotional periods. Once they're gone, they may or may not return depending on customer demand.

Tips for Not Missing Out

Checking in every few weeks helps catch new releases before they disappear. Following official updates or checking the latest seasonal lineup regularly is the easiest way to stay updated.
