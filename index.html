Tim Hortons Seasonal Menu Tracker

Tim Hortons regularly rotates its menu with seasonal and limited-time items, making it easy to miss out if you're not paying attention. This page tracks recent additions and what to expect throughout the year.

Recent Seasonal Additions

Some of the most talked-about recent items include the Chocolate Pistachio lineup and TimsBoost energy drinks — both introduced as limited-time offerings that brought a different flavor experience compared to the regular menu. Since these items don't stick around for long, checking the latest seasonal lineup every so often helps avoid missing out entirely.

How Often Does the Menu Change

Seasonal items typically show up a few times a year, often tied to specific seasons or promotional periods. Once they're gone, they may or may not return depending on customer demand, which makes timing worth paying attention to if there's a particular flavor worth trying.

Tips for Not Missing Out

Checking in every few weeks is usually enough to catch new releases before they disappear.
