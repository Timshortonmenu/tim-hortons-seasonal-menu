Coffee Menu Notes: A Quick Look at Tim Hortons Categories

Tim Hortons is a well-known Canadian coffee and quick-service chain. Its menu is easier to navigate when you think of it in four groups. These notes are an independent overview, not an official page, so always check the in-store board or the official app for current items.

1. Hot and cold drinks

Coffee is the core of the menu. Most locations offer brewed coffee, tea, hot chocolate, espresso drinks, and cold options such as iced coffee and frozen drinks. Seasonal flavours come and go through the year.

2. Donuts and Timbits

Donuts and Timbits (small donut holes) are the grab-and-go favourites. Timbits are sold individually or in boxes, which makes them popular for groups and office coffee runs.

3. Breakfast

Breakfast usually includes hot sandwiches, wraps, and bakery items. Serving hours for breakfast items differ between locations, so it is worth checking before you go.

4. Lunch and snacks

For lunch, expect soups, sandwiches, and wraps, along with lighter snacks. The range can vary by region and by restaurant.

Tips for ordering

Decide on your drink first, then pick a food item that suits the time of day. If you are ordering for a group, a box of Timbits and a few larger drinks is an easy combination.
